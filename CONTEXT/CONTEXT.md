# Media Agent — Domain Language

The shared glossary for the Media Agent pipeline (Pivot.6: AI-generated Tech/AI news Shorts). This file is a glossary only — no implementation detail. Module responsibilities live in `agents.md`; the phase log lives alongside this file in `CONTEXT/`.

## Language

### Content units

**Topic**:
An **on-niche** news item ingested from a feed, awaiting scripting. **On-niche** means the story's center of gravity is AI — a model or research release, or AI shipping inside a product (Apple Intelligence, Copilot) — **or** a major flagship hardware/OS launch (new iPhone, a major iOS version, a flagship GPU). Culture, entertainment, lawsuits and industry drama, and minor/incremental tech are **off-niche** and excluded even when they appear on a tech feed.
_Avoid_: story, article, item.

**Script**:
The narration + per-shot prompts + title an LLM writes from one **Topic**.
_Avoid_: prompt, copy.

**Shot**:
One AI-generated ~4-second video segment. A **Clip** is four **Shots** stitched in order.
_Avoid_: clip-part, segment.

**Clip**:
The finished ~16-second vertical Short — the unit that gets uploaded. Each **Clip** has exactly one **Content kind**.
_Avoid_: video, short (lowercase), render.

**Content kind**:
What a **Clip** is made of: `ai_generated` (current, Pivot.6) or `sourced` (legacy movie-clip). Gates uploader templating and disclosure.
_Avoid_: clip type, format.

**Shot kind**:
Each **Shot** is tagged `real_image` (sourced still + Ken Burns motion) or `ai_video` (Kling transition/atmosphere). A **Hybrid clip** mixes ~2 of each.
_Avoid_: shot type, media type.

**Hybrid clip**:
A **Clip** whose four **Shots** mix both **Shot kinds** — the default Pivot.7 form (~2 **Real-image shots** + ~2 **AI-video shots**, alternating). Still `Content kind = ai_generated`; "hybrid" describes the **Shot kind** mix, not a new **Content kind**.
_Avoid_: mixed clip, real clip.

**Real-image shot**:
A **Shot** built from a **Licensed source** still of a product, logo, or object — never a living person — with Ken Burns motion applied.
_Avoid_: stock photo, slideshow frame.

**Licensed source**:
A rights-cleared origin for a **Real-image shot** still: brand logo APIs, Wikimedia, Openverse. Distinguished from open web-search results, which are not rights-cleared and are excluded from autonomous (auto-published) **Clips**.
_Avoid_: stock, free image, source.

**AI-video shot**:
A **Shot** generated by Kling for abstract/transitional b-roll between real-image shots.
_Avoid_: AI clip, generated segment.

**Stitch**:
The act of combining the four **Shots** in order into one **Clip** — concatenation plus optional crossfade between adjacent **Shots**.
_Avoid_: merge, join, render.

**Shot normalization**:
Conforming every **Shot** to one canonical video format before they are **Stitched**, so heterogeneous **Shots** — e.g. a **Real-image shot** and an **AI-video shot**, which are produced at different resolutions and frame rates — can be combined. Without it, the **Stitch** fails.
_Avoid_: transcode, conform, scaling.

**Synthetic-person frame**:
A **Shot** that depicts an AI-generated human. Constrained by the no-living-individuals rule: a **Shot** prompt must never name a real person (narration may, factual reporting is fine).
_Avoid_: deepfake, fake person.

### Topic selection

**Significance**:
The magnitude of a **Topic** — how major the launch or announcement is, and how major the player behind it. The primary axis for deciding which **Topics** become **Clips**, replacing the retired novelty/tension scoring that rewarded edgy or weird items. A new frontier model from a major lab scores high; an incremental or obscure item scores low.
_Avoid_: novelty, importance, virality.

**Trending corroboration**:
Live external confirmation that a **Topic** is currently hot, read from the Hacker News front page. A **Topic** also trending on HN is boosted in selection. Distinct from **Significance**, which is an intrinsic editorial judgement — corroboration is measured, real-time popularity.
_Avoid_: trending score, popularity, buzz.

### Creative direction

**Hermes director**:
The external **Nous Research Hermes Agent** (a standalone self-improving agent, not a model)
acting as the channel's creative director, running on the `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra:free`
backend. It performs **ideation** (concept/angle for a **Topic**) and **direction** (the
per-**Shot** Kling prompts — "the responses sent to Kling"). It is configured and run
*outside* this repo; it touches the pipeline only by writing **Directed scripts** (ADR-0008).
_Avoid_: Hermes model, director model, the AI.

**Directed script**:
A `scripts` row authored by the **Hermes director** rather than the qwen scripter: `title` +
`shots_json` (tagged hybrid schema) filled, `narration` empty, `status='directed'`. The qwen
scripter later fills narration only. A **Topic** with no **Directed script** falls back to the
full-qwen path. Directed shots are *not* exempt from licensed-sourcing or the
no-living-individuals rule — they pass the same downstream gates. See ADR-0008.
_Avoid_: director brief, Hermes script, AI script.

### Ship lifecycle

**First live ship**:
The first upload of a new **Content kind**. Governed by the two-gate sign-off (see `docs/adr/0001`). Distinct from steady-state daily uploads.

**Mechanics-validation ship**:
A **First live ship** whose purpose is to prove the pipeline end-to-end (upload → disclosure → Content ID → cost reconciliation), not to showcase content quality. Held to "compliant + not embarrassing," not portfolio quality.
_Contrast_: a quality ship, judged on viewer-facing polish.

**Ship gate**:
The T+1h check that the immediate upload path succeeded (disclosure visible, no Content ID claim, cost reconciled). Passing it unblocks the next slice. Marked `[~]`.

**Stability gate**:
The T+48h check that the **Clip** stayed live and clean. Marked `[x]`. Runs concurrently; does not block forward work.

**Review stage**:
The lifecycle position of a **Clip** on its way to publication, *derived* by reconciling which `output/` directory currently holds its file with its DB fields — **Awaiting review** (file in `output/pending/`), **Approved / scheduled** (file in `output/approved/`, `publish_at_utc` set, not yet uploaded), **Published** (`youtube_video_id` set), or **Rejected**. Distinct from `clips.status`, which does not encode the filesystem HITL position — the drag-to-approve gate lives on disk, not in a column.
_Avoid_: clip state, status (when you mean the filesystem position).

**Approve / Reject action**:
The operator decision that advances a **Clip** out of **Awaiting review** — *the same drag-to-approve gate*, whether performed by dragging the MP4 in Explorer or by clicking a button in the dashboard. Both do exactly one thing: move the file `pending/ → approved/` (approve) or `pending/ → rejected/` (reject). It is not a new gate and writes no DB column; the filesystem remains the single source of truth `daily_upload` trusts. Only meaningful while `human_review` is on.
_Avoid_: approval status, approve flag.

**Operator override**:
A dashboard **v3.1** action that mutates a constrained set of **pre-publication** `clips`
fields — **reschedule** (`publish_at_utc` / slot) or **edit title** (`suggested_title` +
`hook`) — and renames the corresponding `output/` file. Distinct from the **Approve / Reject
action**, which moves files only (ADR-0006): an **Operator override** *does* write the DB,
but only for a **Clip** with no `youtube_video_id`, only while holding `data/.weekly_run.lock`,
DB-first-then-rename (ADR-0007), and only on `127.0.0.1`. Triggering `gen_run` and LAN/token
auth are **not** overrides — they are deferred to v3.2.
_Avoid_: edit action, admin action, control.

### Operations & observability

**Run**:
One recorded execution of a scheduled entry point — `gen_run` (`kind='generation'`, the weekly heavy run) or `daily_upload` (`kind='daily'`) — persisted to the `runs` table with `started_at`, `finished_at`, a `success` flag, and a `summary_json` (per-stage counts, or the error string on failure). The dashboard's health view reads the latest **Run** of each kind.
_Avoid_: job, execution, invocation.

**Pipeline health**:
The at-a-glance answer to "is the agent OK right now?", composed from the most recent generation and daily **Runs** (succeeded / failed / not-yet-run), the open **Alerts**, spend against caps, and queue depth. A *derived summary*, not a stored field.
_Avoid_: status, state.

**Alert**:
One appended line in `logs/alerts.md` recording a noteworthy pipeline event — run failure, loudness warning, quota-exceeded, missed-slot recovery, run finished. The dashboard surfaces the most recent **Alerts** newest-first. Distinct from a **Run** outcome: an **Alert** is a discrete event notice, a **Run** is an execution record.
_Avoid_: log, notification, warning (generic).

## Relationships

- A **Topic** produces one **Script**.
- A **Script** produces one **Clip** and its four **Shots**.
- A **Clip** is produced by **Stitching** its four **Shots**; a hybrid mix of **Shot kinds** requires **Shot normalization** first.
- A **Clip** has exactly one **Content kind**; only `ai_generated` carries the synthetic-media disclosure.
- A **First live ship** passes the **Ship gate** before the **Stability gate**.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "The narration names Corti's CEO — does that trip the no-living-individuals rule?"
> **Domain expert:** "No. The rule is about **Shot** prompts, not narration. Narration can state facts about real people. What it can't do is generate a **Synthetic-person frame** *from a prompt that names them* — that's a fake person under a real name."
> **Dev:** "So this clip is fine because the prompt said 'a CEO on a video call', not 'Andreas Cleve'?"
> **Domain expert:** "Right — and because it's a **Mechanics-validation ship**, a generic synthetic face is acceptable as long as disclosure is set."

## Flagged ambiguities

- **"test channel" vs "channel"** — every ship so far targets the YouTube *test* channel. "the channel" without qualification means the test channel until a primary channel is named.
- Pivot.7 adds: a **real_image** `entity` must be a product/logo/object, never a living person (no sourcing identifiable individuals).
- **Dashboard "v2"** — resolved 2026-06-01. The original dashboard grill earmarked "v2" as *approve/reject from the UI* and "v3+" as reschedule/trigger/edit/LAN. The v2 work now bundles that approve/reject write-path **with** a health-first visual redesign; "v2" means both. The deferred controls (reschedule slots, trigger `gen_run`, edit titles, LAN exposure + token auth) are now **v3**.
